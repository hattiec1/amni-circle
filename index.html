What is Amni Circle?

A complete community
event platform — your brand,

What every plan includes

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Your brand, your colors
Your logo, your accent color, your org name on every card and email. Participants see your identity, not ours.
Magic link login
No passwords. Participants click a link in their email and they're in. Works on any device, any browser.
13-zone timezone support
Every virtual event automatically shows the correct local time for participants in the US, Africa, Europe, Asia, and more.
Smart address (14 countries)
Your submission form adapts by country — ZIP codes, Parishes, Postal codes, Digital addresses. No wrong fields.
Multilingual reminders
Participants choose English, Spanish, French, or Hindi. Reminders are sent in their preferred language automatically.
Email marketing integration
Compatible with Constant Contact and Mailchimp — new registrants sync automatically to your list. Weekly digest in Early Season or In-Season mode, two clicks to send. HubSpot available as a custom feature.
Admin portal + review
PIN-protected staff portal. Review submitted sessions — see the flyer and logo before you approve. One click to go live.
Attendance data + CSV export
Know who attended, how many events, which activity types. Download everything for grant reports or board presentations.
Post-session email
After every event, the facilitator sends a personal thank-you to all participants — with upcoming events built right in. 45–65% open rate.
